# Surface: bridgeaide.org (all routes)

Scope: the full marketing site migrated off Wix. Home, About, Projects, Events, 4 event recaps, Team are **Persuade**; Privacy Policy and Accessibility are **Read** sheets in the same world.

Audience and job: high-school students, educators, policymakers/community leaders. Action: join the mailing list (first name + email), register for events when they exist, email info@bridgeaide.org. Proof: four real workshops with dates, places, photos; twelve named student leaders with portraits; the in-progress student bill. Constraints: keep every word of live-site copy and every slug; no invented partners, testimonials, or stats; 501(c)(3) is pending.

Unresolved: mailing-list provider (form posts to a configurable endpoint); hosting target; privacy policy still names Wix.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a drawing set for a bridge between AI development and ethics. Students are the drafters; ethics is the checker's redline. It refuses the category default of blurred neural-net hexagons, stock students at laptops, and three icon cards on white.

OWN-WORLD: Cyanotype blueprint: Prussian-blue ground, off-white linework and lettering, brand steel (#5B87AA) for construction and dimension lines, one red checker's-pencil accent (#c0392b on whiteprint, #ff5c4d on blueprint). Whiteprint (diazo: blue ink on cool white) is the same set in the other print process. Parts: sheet frame and title block, dimension lines with arrowheads, leader callouts with balloons, section hatching, solid = built and dashed = proposed, stencil stamps. Type: Big Shoulders (Chicago-made condensed gothic) for titles, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for prose, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for annotation lettering. Photos print as cyanotypes.

STORY: A visitor learns that Bridge AIDE is student-led and teaches how AI is built together with whether it is fair. They see real workshops, real people, and the bill in progress, then subscribe, register, or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The header bar is 64px: the logo at left, About, Projects, Events, and Team links, and Subscribe at right. Inside the sheet frame, a two-line Big Shoulders headline runs across the top-left nine columns. Below it sit the intro paragraph and two ink-outline actions. The bottom band is a full-width suspension-bridge elevation. Tower A is labelled AI DEVELOPMENT, tower B is labelled ETHICS, and a dimension line reads BRIDGE AIDE. The title block, bottom-right, is the working mailing-list form, and its Subscribe button is the only redline fill. Owner decision, 2026-10-11: the elevation runs columns 1 to 8 with the title block in columns 9 to 12 on the sheet's bottom-right border, chosen over a full-width band so Subscribe stays above the fold at 1280x800. On mobile, everything stacks in order: headline, intro, actions, drawing, title block.

FORM: Engineering drawing set (bridge blueprint), candidate 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed 229eaf53. Signature interaction: the bridge drafts itself in construction order on load (foundations, towers, cables, hangers, deck, dimensions, lettering). A Blueprint/Whiteprint print switch then re-exposes the whole sheet. Motion grammar: lines draw, never bounce; rules draw left to right as sections enter view; reduced motion is static.
- Raise from the timetable rack: annotation lettering at one height. Rank comes from weight, case, and rule, never from scale.
- Raise from the algorave floor: pending work reads as pending. The bill, 501(c)(3), hackathons, spotlight, upcoming events, and the 2025 targets are drawn dashed.
- Raise from the alien understory: luminance is reserved. Redline marks only the single primary action per view and the review marks.
- Raise from the racing league: state is stamped. REGISTRATION CLOSED, RECEIVED, and IN PROGRESS appear as stencil stamps.
- Raise from teletext: a rigid cell grid. Every sheet sits on one 12-column drafting grid with fixed margins.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
