## About (`/about`)

- Title: About Bridge AI Development and Ethics | Student-Led AI Organization
- Description: Learn about Bridge AIDE, a student-led AI organization promoting youth-driven AI ethics and student advocacy in tech to promote AI equity and inclusion.

**About Bridge AIDE**

Bridge AI Development and Ethics is a student-led AI organization fueled by the shared urgency that young people must have a seat at the table in designing and governing artificial intelligence. We are coders, policy nerds, question-askers, and future-builders tired of waiting for a more ethical tech future, so we’re building it ourselves.

We exist because too many AI conversations happen without students in the room. Bridge AIDE creates space for youth to engage critically, creatively, and collaboratively with AI technologies through education, policy, and peer-led exploration. This commitment to youth-driven AI ethics sets us apart in the field.

**Why Ethics, Why Now?**

As AI shapes everything from healthcare to hiring, bias and exclusion can no longer be dismissed as glitches. Ethics must be embedded from the ground up, starting with how we teach and learn about AI. At Bridge AIDE, we don’t separate code from conscience. Our workshops teach students how algorithms work and why they sometimes fail. Learning about AI includes examining who it serves, who it overlooks, and how it can be redesigned for equity, a core focus of our student advocacy in tech.

**Our Organizational Values**

At Bridge AIDE, our core values guide every partnership and project. We’re proud to uphold the following principles:

- Access: We reach students across skill levels, backgrounds, and geographies
- Ethics: Our work centers justice, inclusion, and public accountability
- Youth-Led: Students don’t just participate; we lead the charge for AI equity and inclusion

**Nonprofit Status**

We are currently in the process of becoming a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Our EIN and related documentation will be available as soon as registration is complete. In the meantime, we are committed to full transparency as we grow.

## Projects (`/projects`)

- Title: Projects and AI Education Initiatives | Bridge AI Development and Ethics

**Our Projects: Leading the Way in AI Education Initiatives**

At Bridge AIDE, our AI education initiatives are rooted in action and inquiry. We don’t just teach students what AI is; we support them as they shape how it’s used and governed. Every project reflects our core belief: that young people can be watchdogs, architects, and conscience-keepers of technology.

**Workshops: Code Meets Conscience**

Our student-led AI workshops combine technical skill-building with ethical exploration. Whether students are learning to code with NumPy or unpacking algorithmic bias, every session empowers them to think critically and act responsibly. From foundational AI and machine learning concepts to hands-on model training, our workshops are designed by and for youth.

**Speakers: Real Voices, Real Impact**

We regularly invite guest speakers from academia, activism, and tech industries to engage with students in honest, human conversations about AI. These talks help demystify AI careers and explore ethical challenges and opportunities.

**Policy: Student-Led Advocacy**

We’re currently drafting a student-informed bill aimed at encouraging ethical AI education in public schools. Through research, peer discussion, and collaboration with mentors and policy experts, our team is advancing ethical AI policy projects that ensure AI education is grounded in justice and accountability, not just functionality.

**Hackathons: Still Taking Shape**

While not a current focus, we are exploring how hackathons could support ethical innovation and inclusion. Instead of fast-paced coding sprints, we envision events where ethics, empathy, and real-world impact lead the design process.

**Student Spotlight**

Check back soon to see how our students are launching their own AI policy campaigns and tools to promote accessibility in tech, part of our commitment to AI access and equity programs.

## Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy`), original Wix version

- Title: Privacy Policy | Bridge AI Development and Ethics
- Description: Learn how Bridge Aide collects, uses, and protects your information. We value transparency and your right to privacy.

**Bridge AIDE Privacy Policy**
Effective Date: Wednesday, June 18, 2025

Bridge AIDE values your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, how we use it, and what choices you have.

**Information We Collect**
We collect personal information in two ways:
- Mailing List Sign-Up: First name and email address
- Event Registration: First name, last name, and email address

We also use third-party services (Wix, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console), which may automatically collect information such as device type, browser, IP address, and site usage through cookies.

**How We Use Your Information**
We use the information you provide to:
- Send newsletters or updates (if you signed up for the mailing list)
- Manage event participation (if you registered for an event)

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

**Cookies and Tracking**
Our website uses cookies and tracking technologies via Google services to understand site usage and improve performance. You can control cookie settings in two ways:
- Via your browser settings
- Through the cookie banner on our website, where you can opt in or out of non-essential cookies

**Youth Privacy Notice**
Some of our users may be under 18. We take special care to collect only the information necessary to provide services like event sign-up or newsletter communication. We do not knowingly collect more information than needed or target advertising based on age.

If you are under 13, please obtain a parent or guardian’s permission before submitting your information.

**Your Rights**
You can:
- Request access to your personal information
- Ask us to delete your information

To do so, please email us at info@bridgeaide.org.

**Data Storage**
Our site is hosted in the United States, and data is stored securely on servers based in the U.S.

**Changes to This Policy**
We may update this Privacy Policy as our organization grows or our services change. Updates will be posted here with a new effective date.

## Accessibility Statement (`/accessibility`)

- Title: Accessibility Statement | Bridge AI Development and Ethics
- Description: Bridge Aide is committed to making our site accessible to all. Read how we’re working to provide an inclusive digital experience.

**Accessibility Statement**

At Bridge AI Development and Ethics, we believe ethical technology starts with inclusive design, and that includes our website.

We’re committed to making our digital spaces accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. Our goal is to ensure that all visitors can engage fully with our content, events, and resources regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology.

**What We're Doing**
We aim to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards. This means we’re working to:
- Provide clear, consistent navigation across our site
- Use descriptive alt text for images and meaningful link text
- Ensure readable contrast and scalable text
- Make forms and buttons screen-reader-friendly
- Design with keyboard navigation in mind

**Still Encountering a Barrier?**
We’re a student-led organization, and we’re learning as we go. If you experience any difficulty accessing our content or have suggestions for how we can improve, we want to hear from you.

Please reach out to us at info@bridgeaide.org with:
- The page or content you were trying to access
- The issue you encountered
- Any assistive technology or browser you’re using (if applicable)

We’ll do our best to fix the issue promptly and keep learning along the way.
